Five new pages cover what’s new in Rent Party: table records, Online now and matches, reputation and streaks, coins, and the community feed.

- [Table records](/table-records): keep a real-board game’s deeds, bucks and box between nights, share it with friends, and bring it online.
- [Online now and matches](/online-now): see who’s around, hide yourself, challenge a friend or find a match at a similar level.
- [Reputation and streaks](/reputation): what your score counts, the 10 levels, “New”, and the daily streak badges.
- [Coins](/coins): coin packs on the website, coin tables, coin challenges and sending coins. Coins are in-app only, with no cash value.
- [Community](/community): posts, who sees them, likes, comments, reporting and blocking.

[Online play](/online-play#offline) now explains the 2-minute offline rule for live games.
