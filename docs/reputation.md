Your reputation grows when you finish what you start, stay connected and come back day after day. It starts with your first online game.

## What does the score count? {#score}

Your score is out of 100, and it’s made of four parts:

| Part | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Finishing games | Games you play to the end, and games you leave early. |
| Staying connected | How steady your connection is in online games. |
| Coming back each day | Your daily streak. |
| Time on Rent Party | How long you’ve had your account. |

Each part counts for a share of every 100 points. Your [profile](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/profile) shows the breakdown under **How your score is made**, and only you see that part.

## What do the levels mean? {#levels}

Your score sets your level, from 1 to 10. The level shows on your profile, your public player page and your card on [Online now](./online-now.md).

Other players see your level and the reputation card: games finished, games left early, connection and your streaks. For them it reads as how reliably you play games to the end.

Levels matter in two places:

- [Find a match](./online-now.md#find-a-match) pairs you with someone at a similar level.
- A [coin table](./coins.md#coin-tables) can open at a set level, and its card shows which one.

## What does “New” mean? {#new}

You’ve played fewer than 5 games so far, so your level is still settling. The label goes once you’ve played 5.

## How does the daily streak work? {#streak}

Your daily streak counts the days in a row you come back to Rent Party signed in. Your reputation card shows the current streak and your longest one.

Two badges come from it:

| Badge | How you earn it |
|---|---|
| A week of game nights | Open Rent Party 7 days in a row. |
| A month of game nights | Open Rent Party 30 days in a row. |

Your badges are on [Achievements](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/achievements).

## What counts against it? {#left-early}

Leaving games early and dropping out of them. A live game also ends for you if you’re offline too long: [Online play](./online-play.md#offline) has the 2-minute rule.

Coin tables count toward your rating and reputation, like any other online game.
