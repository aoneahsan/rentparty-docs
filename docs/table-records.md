Playing on a real board over several nights? Keep the deeds, the bucks and the box in a table record, so nobody has to remember who owned what.

Open [Table records](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/table-records) and choose **New record**. Give it a name of 2 to 40 characters, something the table will recognize, like “Kitchen table, Friday nights”.

## What goes in a record? {#what-goes-in}

Four steps, in order. Each change is saved and logged.

| Step | What you enter |
|---|---|
| Players | Who’s at the table, 2 to 6 people, since a real board seats 6. Each has a name and a token. Link someone to their account so they can see the record and take their seat online. Only your friends can be linked. |
| Deeds | Tick each deed a person holds. Buildings and mortgages go on the street itself. |
| Bucks | Each person’s bucks, or leave it blank. Blank reads as “not entered”. |
| The box | The houses and hotels left in the box. The record tells you what the box should hold by the deeds, and **Use these numbers** fills them in. |

Your board calls a color group something else? Choose **Rename** on the group and use the name on your own board, 1 to 24 characters. **Use our name** puts it back.

## What does the record check? {#checks}

Before a game can go online, everything has to add up. The record lists anything to fix, and **Go to it** takes you there:

- A deed has one owner.
- Buildings need the whole color group, held by one person.
- A group with buildings can’t have a mortgaged street.
- Streets in a group can differ by one building at most.
- The deeds can’t hold more houses or hotels than a full box.

When it all adds up, you see *Everything adds up.*

## What does the log show? {#log}

Every entry and every edit, newest first: when, who, what changed, and the before and after. Nothing in the log can be changed or removed. Use **Show** to see one person’s entries.

## Can you pick it up next time? {#keep}

Yes. Choose **Keep as a record**, and pick it up again next game night.

**Close this record** makes it read-only for everyone. You can reopen it later, and the log stays.

Signed out, records stay on this device. Sign in to share them with the people at your table.

## How do you share a record? {#share}

Under **Who can see this record**, choose **Add a friend**, then **Invite**. Friends you add can see the record and add entries, and every entry lands in the log.

Your friend finds it under **Shared with you** on their Table records list, and chooses **Accept** or **Not now**. You can remove someone at any time, and they can’t see the record any more.

## How do you bring a game online? {#bring-online}

Choose **Bring online** on the record. Keep playing from wherever everyone is: the board starts exactly where your table left it.

- Only the person who started the record can bring it online.
- Every seat needs its person, linked to their account, and each of them needs to accept before the game can start.
- Pick who rolls first, then choose **Start online game**. Everyone gets their seat back.

The deeds, buildings, bucks and box come online as recorded. The start screen shows what comes online. A seat with no bucks entered starts with the amount shown there, and both decks are shuffled fresh.

It’s a friends’ game, and it’s unrated. No coins go in and none come out. The rest of [online play](./online-play.md) works as usual, pausing and votes included.

Once the game is online, the record shows **Moved online** and is read-only.
