Game night runs in your browser or the Android app. Keep the rules a tab away.

The browser game works on phones, tablets and computers, and the Android app is on Google Play. Start either one with [the first-game guide](./intro.md#start-a-game). When a release asks you to update, [New versions](./new-versions.md) explains what happens to the game already at your table.
