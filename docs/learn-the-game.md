Learn the game is the real Rent Party game screen on a demo table, with a coach that walks you through every control in 16 steps and then takes you to set up a practice game. Open it at [rentparty.aoneahsan.com/learn](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/learn). Nothing on the demo table is saved.

## What does Learn the game show? {#steps}

Every control on the game screen, in the order a turn uses them:

1. **This is the table:** a demo game called Sunday night, and you’re Noor, the rocket.
2. **Who’s playing:** one chip per player, showing their bucks and how many deeds they hold.
3. **Roll the dice:** doubles mean you go again, and 3 in a row send you to Jail.
4. **Buy a street:** buy it at the printed price, or turn it down and it goes to auction.
5. **Bid at auction:** everyone can bid, you included. A pass is final.
6. **Latest move:** who paid whom; press it for the whole game log.
7. **Your deeds:** press any street on the board to read its deed.
8. **Manage what you own:** every build and every mortgage happens here.
9. **Houses and hotels:** own the whole color set and build evenly. A hotel comes after 4 houses.
10. **Mortgage and pay back:** the bank pays you half the printed price, and paying it back costs that plus 10%.
11. **Trade:** offer deeds or bucks to anyone at the table.
12. **The game log:** every move so far, newest at the bottom.
13. **Moments:** the big ones pop up on the table, then leave by themselves.
14. **End your turn:** the dice pass to the next player.
15. **Pause the game:** pause saves your seat.
16. **You’re ready:** come back any time, or play a practice game.

## How do I open Learn the game? {#open}

Press **Learn the game** in the site header, in the footer’s Play group or in your account menu, whichever is closest when you want a refresher. In a game, open the pause menu and choose **How the game screen works**.

It covers the game screen only. Every other page has a [tour of its own](./tours-and-walkthrough.md#page-tour), from the button in the header.

The first time you open a game, a short note offers it: *Your first roll can wait. Learn the game screen first.* Choose **Show me** to start. The note appears once in each browser and once for your account, so a second device won’t show it again.

## Can I skip steps or leave? {#controls}

Yes, at any step. **Next** and **Back** move one step, and **All steps** lists all 16 so you can jump straight to one. **Skip** or **Close** ends it and takes you to [How to play](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/how-to-play). Escape does the same.

Each step puts a ring around one control, and the table stays live, so you can press what the step points at and see what it does.

## Is anything on the demo table saved? {#saved}

No. Sunday night is a demo with Noor, Maya, Sam and Pip the CPU character at the table, and nothing you do there is saved, so roll and build all you like.

## What happens at the last step? {#done}

**Play a practice game** takes you to set up a practice game against a CPU character, where the next roll counts.

Ready when you are. [Learn the game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/learn).
