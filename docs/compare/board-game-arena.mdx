Nobody has to leave Board Game Arena. It holds hundreds of games and Rent Party is one. Come over for the property-trading night, and keep your account there for everything else.

Here, no plan is needed to open a table, and the game does pass and play on one phone and CPU characters as well as online rooms.

## Your Premium and your history \{#move}

Both stay on Board Game Arena.

## Turn-based, the way your group already plays \{#table}

1. Sign in with Google on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and choose Online.
2. Pick Over days and a deadline of 8, 12, 24 or 48 hours [per turn](../online-play.md#modes).
3. Send the [invite](../online-play.md#invites). Your plan doesn't decide whether you can open a table; [its limits](../plans-and-pricing.md#limits) decide how many you run at once.

## A different game every week? \{#stay}

Then Board Game Arena is the better home, with real-time and turn-based tables already set up for hundreds of them.
