Your group doesn't have to leave Board Game Arena to try this. It holds hundreds of games and Rent Party is one. Come over for the property-trading night, and keep your account there for everything else.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Your Premium plan and your game history stay on Board Game Arena.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Sign in with Google on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and choose Online.
2. Your group knows turn-based play already, so pick Over days and a deadline of 8, 12, 24 or 48 hours [per turn](../online-play.md#modes).
3. Send the [invite](../online-play.md#invites). Your plan doesn't decide whether you can open a table; [its limits](../plans-and-pricing.md#limits) decide how many you run at once.

## Who should stay on Board Game Arena? \{#stay}

Groups who want a different board game every week, over days, with real-time and turn-based tables already set up for that.
