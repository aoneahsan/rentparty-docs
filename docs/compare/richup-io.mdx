Richup.io and Rent Party both open in a browser, so this is the shortest move on the list. What changes is who sits down. Richup.io is at its best when you want a public table of strangers right now. Rent Party adds one phone passed round a couch, CPU characters when you're on your own, ranked games, and a seat that's saved while the table pauses for days.

## What stays on Richup.io \{#move}

Coins and board maps from its store. Rent Party has its own shop and its own coins, and it can't read theirs.

## Three ways in \{#table}

- **On your own.** Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and pick [a CPU character](../how-to-play/cpu-characters.md) on Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert. No account.
- **On one couch.** Pass-and-play, also with no account.
- **Friends far away.** Sign in with Google and start a room. [Who can find it](../online-play.md#start-a-room) is your call: invite only, friends, or anyone.

## When a stranger's table is the point \{#stay}

Stay on Richup.io for an instant public table or a store of board maps. Our public rooms depend on who's online.

## Both tables, dated \{#facts}

Richup.io's pages didn't say whether it has ranked games, pass-and-play on one phone, computer players, turns spread over days, pausing, making your own board, or Android and iOS apps, so those rows are left out.
