Both games open in a browser, so nobody installs anything either way. What changes is who's at the table: Rent Party also does one phone on a couch, and CPU characters when nobody else is around.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Coins and board maps from Richup.io's store stay on Richup.io. Rent Party has its own shop and its own coins, and it can't read theirs.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). No account needed for pass-and-play or CPU characters.
2. On your own? Pick [a CPU character](../how-to-play/cpu-characters.md) on Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert.
3. With friends far away? Sign in with Google and start a room. [Who can find it](../online-play.md#start-a-room) is your call: invite only, friends, or anyone.

## Who should stay on Richup.io? \{#stay}

Anyone who wants a public table with strangers right now, or a store of board maps to pick from. Our public rooms depend on who's online.
