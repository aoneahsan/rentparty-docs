Weigh the maps first. Business Tour lets you draw your own, and Rent Party has no board editor yet; our boards come from the game and its city editions. What you get instead is a browser with no download, turns that can wait days, up to 8 at one table, and a seat kept while the game is paused.

## Maps, gold and tournaments \{#move}

All of it stays in Business Tour. Rent Party can't import any of it.

## A first game in the browser \{#table}

1. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). Nothing to download.
2. Pick your [board](../city-boards.md) and [house rules](../how-to-play/house-rules.md). Every board plays by the same rules.
3. Going longer than a night? Sign in, choose Over days, and the game [waits for each turn](../online-play.md#modes).

## Who Business Tour still suits \{#stay}

Map makers, anyone who wants to replay a match from the same dice seed, and players chasing daily tournaments on Steam or an iPhone.
