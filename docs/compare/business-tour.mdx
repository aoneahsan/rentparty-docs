Say goodbye to your maps first: Business Tour lets you draw your own, and Rent Party has no board editor yet. Our boards come from the game and its city editions.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Maps, gold and tournament standings stay in Business Tour. Rent Party can't import any of them.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) in a browser, with no download.
2. Pick your [board](../city-boards.md) and [house rules](../how-to-play/house-rules.md). Every board plays by the same rules.
3. For more than a night, sign in, choose Over days and let the game [wait for each turn](../online-play.md#modes).

## Who should stay on Business Tour? \{#stay}

Anyone who wants to draw maps, replay a match from the same dice seed, or chase daily tournaments on Steam or an iPhone.
