Quadropoly is a solo game at heart, with eight levels of trained AI opponents on Android. Rent Party has four levels, Easy to Expert, played by CPU characters with names, personalities and opinions. The bigger change is that your friends can sit down too, in a private room or round one phone.

Rent Party isn't on Google Play yet. On an Android phone it runs in the browser.

## Your Quadropoly progress \{#move}

It stays there. We can't read it.

## Start solo, then bring a friend \{#table}

1. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and pick CPU characters. No account, and no signal needed once it's on your device.
2. Choose [who you're up against](../how-to-play/cpu-characters.md): Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert.
3. Rather beat a friend? [Start a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room) and send the invite.

## Keep Quadropoly if you play alone \{#stay}

Solo Android players who want eight AI levels, hundreds of house rules and replays of top players to learn from are better off there.
