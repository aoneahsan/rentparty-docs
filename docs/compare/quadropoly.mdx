If you're mostly here for the AI opponents, know this first: Quadropoly has eight levels and we have four. Ours have names, personalities and opinions. The bigger change is that your friends can sit down too.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Your Quadropoly progress stays there, and we can't read it.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and pick CPU characters. No account, no signal needed once it's on your device.
2. Choose [who you're up against](../how-to-play/cpu-characters.md): Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert.
3. When you'd rather beat a friend, [start a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room) and send the invite.

## Who should stay on Quadropoly? \{#stay}

Solo Android players who want eight AI levels, hundreds of house rules and replays of top players to learn from.
