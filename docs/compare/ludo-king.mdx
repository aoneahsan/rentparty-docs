Everyone in your family already knows Ludo. Rent Party has more to learn — streets, deeds, rent, auctions — and a longer evening to fill. In return: no third-party ads at the table, ranked games when you want them, and a game that waits days if dinner runs long.

## Teaching the family \{#table}

1. Show them [Learn the game](../learn-the-game.md). It walks through a demo table one step at a time.
2. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play), choose Pass-and-play and hand the phone round. No account needed.
3. Out of evening? The game saves on the device, so you can [pick it up later](../how-to-play/pausing.md).

## Your Ludo King coins \{#move}

They stay in Ludo King.

## When a ten-minute race is the plan \{#stay}

Ludo King does that best, with friends a tap away on Android or iPhone and a game everyone already knows.
