Everyone in your family already knows Ludo. Rent Party has more to learn: streets, deeds, rent, auctions. Give the first game a little time, or teach it with the demo table first.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Your Ludo King account and coins stay in Ludo King.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Show the family [Learn the game](../learn-the-game.md). It walks through a demo table a step at a time.
2. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and choose Pass-and-play. Hand the phone around; no account needed.
3. Run out of evening? Your game saves on the device, so [pick it up later](../how-to-play/pausing.md).

## Who should stay on Ludo King? \{#stay}

A family that wants a ten-minute race everyone already knows, with friends a tap away on Android or iPhone.
