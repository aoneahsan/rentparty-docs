Get Rent (Landlord GO) has you buy the real cafe down your street and rank against your city. Rent Party is one table, your own friends, two dice everyone can see, and no third-party ads. They're different games more than rivals, so keep both if you like.

## Who's playing tonight? \{#table}

- **A few of you, one phone.** Choose [Pass-and-play](../intro.md#ways-to-play) on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and hand it round.
- **Nobody around.** CPU characters fill the seats.
- **Everyone in a different city.** Sign in and [send an invite](../online-play.md#invites).

## Your buildings and rankings \{#move}

They stay in Get Rent, and nothing here touches them.

## Why you might keep Get Rent \{#stay}

Owning real places on a real map and ranking against your city, on an iPhone, in your own time. Rent Party has none of that.
