These are different games more than they're rivals. Get Rent (Landlord GO) has you buying the real cafe down your street. Rent Party is a board, two dice and whoever you can talk into a game.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Your buildings and rankings stay in Get Rent, and you can keep playing it alongside Rent Party.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Round up two to eight people. Everyone on one phone? Choose [Pass-and-play](../intro.md#ways-to-play) on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play).
2. Nobody around? CPU characters fill the seats.
3. Everyone in different cities? Sign in and [send an invite](../online-play.md#invites).

## Who should stay on Get Rent? \{#stay}

Anyone who enjoys owning real places on a real map and ranking against their city, on an iPhone, in their own time.
