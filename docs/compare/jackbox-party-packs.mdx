Keep your packs. A Jackbox night is quick drawing and trivia rounds, one screen and everyone's phone as the controller. A Rent Party night is one long game of streets, haggling and rent, on phones alone, free for every seat, and it can stop and carry on another day.

## Two ways to set it up \{#table}

- **Everyone in one room.** Pass one phone around with [Pass-and-play](../intro.md#ways-to-play). No TV, no accounts.
- **Everyone on their own phone.** Sign in with Google on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and share the [room code](../online-play.md#invites).

Getting late? [Pause the game](../how-to-play/pausing.md) and pick it up another evening.

## The packs you bought \{#move}

Still yours. There's nothing to carry between the two.

## Nights that suit Jackbox better \{#stay}

A crowd that wants quick rounds on a TV, where someone can join for ten minutes and leave. A Rent Party game asks for longer than that.

## Dated facts \{#facts}

Jackbox's pages didn't say whether the packs have ranked games, pass-and-play, computer players, turns spread over days, pausing, looks to collect, making your own board, Android or iOS apps, or third-party ads, so those rows are left out.
