Keep your packs. A Jackbox night is quick rounds of drawing and trivia, with one screen and everyone's phone as the controller. A Rent Party night is one long game of streets, haggling and rent, on phones alone.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. There's nothing to carry over between the two, and the packs you bought are still yours.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Everyone in one room? Pass one phone around with [Pass-and-play](../intro.md#ways-to-play). No TV, no accounts.
2. Everyone on their own phone? Sign in with Google on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and share the [room code](../online-play.md#invites).
3. Getting late? [Pause the game](../how-to-play/pausing.md) and carry on another day.

## Who should stay with Jackbox? \{#stay}

A crowd that wants quick rounds on a TV, where someone can join for ten minutes and leave. A Rent Party game asks for longer than that.
