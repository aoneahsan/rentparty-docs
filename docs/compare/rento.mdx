Rento runs on phones, PCs and consoles. Rent Party opens in a browser, so your crew's first step is a link instead of a download.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Coins and boards you bought in Rento stay in Rento, and we have no way to read them. You start at Rent Party with a fresh account and a full bank.

## How do you get the crew to a table? \{#table}

1. Sign in with Google on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). You only need the account for online play.
2. Choose Online, then Live for tonight or Over days if nobody can agree on a time.
3. Send the [game invite](../online-play.md#invites). Your friends open it in their browser and take a seat.

Rento's tables go up to 8 in real time. A Rent Party room opens with 6 seats, and the host can raise it to 8.

## Who should stay on Rento? \{#stay}

Anyone whose crew plays on an Xbox, a Switch or from the App Store. Rent Party has no console version and no iOS app; on an iPhone you play in the browser.
