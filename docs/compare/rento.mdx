Rento's crew plays on phones, PCs and consoles, up to 8 at a live table. Rent Party is a link instead of a download. It opens in a browser, a game can wait days for the next roll, and there are no third-party ads at the table.

## Coins and boards from Rento \{#move}

They stay in Rento, and we have no way to read them. You start here with a fresh account and a full bank.

## Getting the Rento crew seated \{#table}

1. Sign in with Google on [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). The account is only for online play.
2. Choose Online. Live is for tonight. Over days is for when nobody can agree on a time.
3. Send the [game invite](../online-play.md#invites). Friends open it in their browser and take a seat.

Rento's live tables take 8. A Rent Party room opens with 6 seats, and the host can raise it to 8.

## If the crew plays on an Xbox or a Switch \{#stay}

Stay with Rento. Rent Party has no console version and no iOS app; on an iPhone you play in the browser.
