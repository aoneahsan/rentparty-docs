Nothing moves. A board game keeps no records you'd need to carry over, so moving to Rent Party means getting your people to a table. Each guide below says how, and who's better off staying put.

Every price and feature row was read on the other game's own pages. Last checked November 7, 2026. For the side-by-side view with both games' deeds, see [Compare on the Rent Party site](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/compare).

## Which game are you coming from? \{#guides}

- [Rento](./rento.mdx): on phones, PCs and consoles
- [Richup.io](./richup-io.mdx): another browser table
- [Rumble Avenue](./rumble-avenue.mdx): a browser table with no account
- [Business Tour](./business-tour.mdx): on Steam and iPhone, with a map editor
- [Quadropoly](./quadropoly.mdx): solo on Android, with eight AI levels
- [Get Rent (Landlord GO)](./get-rent.mdx): real buildings on a real map
- [Board Game Arena](./board-game-arena.mdx): hundreds of licensed games
- [Tabletopia](./tabletopia.mdx): a virtual table with an editor
- [Tabletop Simulator](./tabletop-simulator.mdx): a 3D sandbox for PC
- [The Jackbox Party Packs](./jackbox-party-packs.mdx): quick rounds on a shared screen
- [Ludo King](./ludo-king.mdx): a ten-minute family race

## What don't we have yet? \{#limits}

Better you hear it from us. There's no board editor. There's no Android app on Google Play yet, and no iOS app; you play in a phone's browser. There are no third-party ads, but a small panel promotes our other apps on some pages.

Then start where you like: [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play).
