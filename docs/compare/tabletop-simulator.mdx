Tabletop Simulator asks every player to buy a copy and run it on a PC. Rent Party asks for a phone browser. The rules run themselves, too, so nobody flips the table over an argument about the rent.

## Mods and saved tables \{#move}

Workshop mods and saved tables stay in Tabletop Simulator. With no board editor here yet, a table you built can't come along.

## Send the group one link \{#table}

1. Share [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). Nobody buys anything before sitting down.
2. The host signs in with Google and [starts a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room). Everyone else playing online signs in too.
3. Short on people? [CPU characters](../how-to-play/cpu-characters.md) take the empty seats.

## PC groups who build \{#stay}

Stay on Tabletop Simulator if you want any board game you can imagine on one 3D table, and you like building and sharing your own.

## The facts, dated \{#facts}

Tabletop Simulator's pages didn't say whether it has ranked games, computer players, turns spread over days, looks to collect, play in the browser, Android or iOS apps, or third-party ads, so those rows are left out.
