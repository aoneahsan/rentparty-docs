Nobody has to own a copy, or a PC. A phone browser is enough, and the rules run themselves, so nobody flips the table to settle an argument about the rent.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Workshop mods and saved tables stay in Tabletop Simulator. There's no board editor here yet, so a table you built can't come along.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Send your group the link to [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). There's nothing to buy before you sit down.
2. Whoever hosts signs in with Google and [starts a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room). Everyone else who plays online signs in too.
3. Short on people? [CPU characters](../how-to-play/cpu-characters.md) fill the empty seats.

## Who should stay on Tabletop Simulator? \{#stay}

Groups on PCs who want any board game they can imagine on one 3D table, and who like building and sharing their own.
