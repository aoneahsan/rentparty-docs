Rumble Avenue never asks who you are. Rent Party only asks when you go online. Pass-and-play and CPU games need no account; online play needs a Google sign-in, because that's how your seat stays yours for days. On top of that you get ranked games, looks to collect, and a game that pauses and picks up days later.

## No account, same as before \{#table}

Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and pick CPU characters or Pass-and-play. Neither asks you to sign in.

## When friends are somewhere else \{#online}

1. Sign in with Google and [start a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room).
2. Can't all sit down at once? Choose Over days, and the game [waits for the next turn](../online-play.md#modes).

## Anything to carry over? \{#move}

No. There's nothing on Rumble Avenue we could read, so you start fresh.

## Rather never sign in? \{#stay}

Then Rumble Avenue suits you better: friends or computer players at a browser table, with no account ever.

## The facts on both \{#facts}

Rumble Avenue's pages didn't say whether it has ranked games, pass-and-play, turns spread over days, pausing, looks to collect, making your own board, Android or iOS apps, or third-party ads, so those rows are left out.
