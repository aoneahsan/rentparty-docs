Rumble Avenue seats you with no account at all. Rent Party matches that for pass-and-play and CPU games. Online play needs a Google sign-in, because that's how your seat stays yours for days.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. You're not leaving anything behind that we could read, so start fresh.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) and pick CPU characters or Pass-and-play. No sign-in for either.
2. When you want friends in other places, sign in with Google and [start a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room).
3. Choose Over days if the crew can't all sit down at once. The game [waits for the next turn](../online-play.md#modes).

## Who should stay on Rumble Avenue? \{#stay}

Anyone who wants friends or computer players at a browser table without ever making an account.
