On a virtual table, somebody has to know the rules and keep everyone honest. Rent Party runs the bank, charges the rent and holds the auction itself. It also brings CPU characters, ranked games and turns that wait days. You give up the freedom of a sandbox, and its editor: there's no board editor here yet.

## Games you built in Tabletopia \{#move}

They stay there. We can't load them.

## Learn it, then sit down \{#table}

1. Skim [the rules](../how-to-play/rules.md), or play a demo table a step at a time in [Learn the game](../learn-the-game.md).
2. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). Pass-and-play and CPU games need no account.
3. For friends elsewhere, sign in and [start a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room).

## A whole shelf of games \{#stay}

Tabletopia is the place for licensed games on one table, or for building and playtesting a game of your own.
