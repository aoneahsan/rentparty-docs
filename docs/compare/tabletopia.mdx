On a virtual table, somebody has to know the rules and keep everyone honest. Here, the game runs the bank, charges the rent and holds the auction. You give up the freedom of a sandbox, and the editor with it: Rent Party has no board editor yet.

## Is there anything to move? \{#move}

No. Games you built in Tabletopia's editor stay there. We can't load them.

## How do you get a table going? \{#table}

1. Skim [the rules](../how-to-play/rules.md), or open [Learn the game](../learn-the-game.md) and play a demo table a step at a time.
2. Open [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play). Pass-and-play and CPU games need no account.
3. For friends elsewhere, sign in and [start a room](../online-play.md#start-a-room).

## Who should stay on Tabletopia? \{#stay}

Anyone who wants a whole shelf of licensed games on one table, or wants to build and playtest a game of their own.
