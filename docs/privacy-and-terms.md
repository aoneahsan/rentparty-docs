These are short guides to the game’s legal pages, not the pages themselves. The pages on rentparty.aoneahsan.com are the ones that apply, and each shows the date it was last updated.

## Privacy {#privacy}

We don’t sell your personal data, and you can delete your account from Settings whenever you like. The privacy page says what Rent Party does keep about you, why, which services help run the game, how long each thing stays and how you change it.

A few answers from it:

- Rent Party shows no third-party ads. A small panel can promote the team’s own other apps.
- Pass-and-play and CPU games are saved only on your device. The team never receives those saves.
- Rent Party is for anyone aged 13 and over, and the game’s own content is family-friendly.

[Read the privacy policy](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/privacy).

## Terms {#terms}

These terms are the rules for using Rent Party, on the website at rentparty.aoneahsan.com and in the Android app. By playing, you agree to them. They cover:

- who can use Rent Party;
- what’s free, what plans add, and how buying a plan and a Family plan work;
- what happens when a plan ends;
- what bucks and coins are worth, and what friend invites earn;
- fair play, leaving games and missed turns;
- what you can say in names, rooms and chat, and how reports and blocks work;
- what happens when someone breaks these rules;
- who owns the game, and whether it can change or go offline;
- who’s responsible if something goes wrong, and which law applies;
- how you’ll know the terms changed, and how to reach the team.

[Read the terms](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/terms).

## Permissions {#permissions}

The only permission prompt you’ll see in Rent Party is for notifications, and it comes only after you tap to turn alerts on. Say no and the game plays the same.

The permissions page lists every permission the Android app and the website use, what each is for, when you’re asked and what happens if you refuse. [See the permissions](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/permissions).

## Deleting your account {#delete-account}

You can delete your Rent Party account at any time from Settings, on the website or in the Android app. Deletion is immediate and can’t be undone. The Delete account page lists what’s deleted at once and what stays, and [Your account](./account.md#delete-account) sums it up.

[Go to the Delete account page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/delete-account).

## How do you reach the team? {#contact}

For privacy and legal requests, the privacy page gives the address to write to. For everything else, write to us from the [Help page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/help).
