New here? Rent Party shows you around two ways. A page tour walks through the page you’re on, and [How Rent Party works](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/how-rent-party-works) covers the whole game in six screens. It takes about a minute, and you can leave at any step.

## How do I start a page tour? {#page-tour}

Press **Take a tour of this page** in the header. On a phone it’s in the bar at the top of the screen. The first time you open a page that has a tour, a short note points at the button: *New here? Take a quick tour of this page.* Choose **Show me** to start, or **Dismiss**. The note shows once per page.

## Which pages have a tour? {#pages}

| Page | What the tour shows |
|---|---|
| [Home](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/) | Every game can pause, the ways to play, how a game night goes, and the rivals |
| [Play](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play) | Pick your kind of game night, pass-and-play, Join a room, Install Rent Party |
| [Your games](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/games) | Every game you started, sorted by how you played it |
| [Play online](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/online) | Start a room, join with a code, public rooms |
| [Shop](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/shop) | Tokens, boards, dice and frames, bought with the coins you earn playing |
| [Leaderboard](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/leaderboard) | Where you stand, and how ratings work |
| [Profile](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/profile) | Your table name, what you have equipped, your stats |
| [Settings](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/settings) | Your account, alerts, how the game looks, and your data |

## Can I skip steps or leave the tour? {#controls}

Yes, at any step. **Next** and **Back** move one step, and **All steps** lists every step so you can jump straight to one. **Skip tour** or **Close the tour** ends it, and so does Escape or a press on the dimmed page. On a keyboard, the arrow keys move between steps. On a phone, each step opens as a card at the bottom of the screen.

## What is How Rent Party works? {#walkthrough}

A quick walk through the game, six screens long:

1. **Pick your kind of game night:** online with friends, pass-and-play on one phone, or CPU characters with names, personalities and opinions.
2. **Roll, buy, build:** the classic property-trading rules, applied for you. Nobody has to be the banker.
3. **Haggle at the table:** offer deeds, bucks or both to anyone at the table.
4. **Pause any game:** life calls? Pause the game and pick it up days later.
5. **Bring your crew online:** share a link, a QR code or the six-character room code.
6. **Dress up your game:** coins buy looks, never luck.

Open it from **How Rent Party works** in the site footer or in your account menu. It’s also on the last step of onboarding: *New to Rent Party? See how it works.*

Ready to play? [Start a game](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/play).
