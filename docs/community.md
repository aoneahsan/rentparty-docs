[Community](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/community) is for game-night stories, rematch calls and the odd building tip from the people you play with.

## How do you post? {#post}

Under **Write a post**, say how game night went. You can use bold, italics and a bulleted list, and link a game or a [table record](./table-records.md). Choose who sees it, then **Post**.

There’s a character limit, and the box counts as you type.

## Who sees your post? {#audience}

You choose for each post:

| Who sees it | Means |
|---|---|
| Friends | Your friends, on their **Friends** tab. |
| Everyone | Everyone on Rent Party, on the **Everyone** tab. |

Each post shows which one it went to.

## How do likes and comments work? {#likes-comments}

Choose **Like** on a post. Choose **Comment** to read the comments and write your own, then **Reply**.

You can delete your own posts and comments. A deleted post has an **Undo**.

## How do you report a post or block someone? {#report-block}

Open the post’s menu, the **More** button, and choose:

- **Report**, for a post or a comment that breaks the rules in the [Terms](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/terms). Pick a reason and add details if you like.
- **Block**, with the person’s name. Posts from people you’ve blocked never show here.

If the team hides one of your posts, you see why on the post, and only you can see it.

## Why didn’t your post go through? {#refused}

Your words stay in the box with the reason under it:

| You see | Why |
|---|---|
| You’ve posted a lot this hour. | There’s an hourly limit on posts and comments. Try again a little later. |
| That didn’t go through. Try different words. | It has a word Rent Party doesn’t allow. |
| Posting and comments are off while your chat is muted. | A mute covers posts too, until the date shown. |
| Posting is off while your account is suspended. | A suspended account can’t post. |
| That post isn’t here any more. | It was deleted while you were writing. |

[Friends, blocks and reports](./account.md#friends-and-safety) covers blocking and reporting everywhere else in the game.
