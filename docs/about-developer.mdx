Ahsan built Rent Party for nights with family and loved ones that end in too much laughter. The Zaions team made the table, the streets and the way back to an unfinished game.

[Visit Ahsan’s portfolio](https://aoneahsan.com) · [Meet Zaions](https://zaions.com)

## Why this game? \{#why-this-game}

Play friends at a Ranked or Friendly Table, pass one phone around, or face a CPU character. Pick your own board look, deed and avatar. The point is the company around the board.

## How did it come together? \{#journey}

- **October 6, 2026:** the web game opened.
- **November 20, 2026:** the Android listing was verified on Google Play.

Online tables, city editions and saved games grew around the same promise: a night together can wait for the next roll.

## What did the team build? \{#what-the-team-built}

Rent Party has original boards, deeds, characters and game-night rhythm. A table can run online over days or around one device in one sitting. Rules and account choices live in these player docs, so you can check a detail without leaving the table behind.
