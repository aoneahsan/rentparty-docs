Set one Android app offline without taking the whole phone offline.

## What does NetCage do? \{#netcage-fact-1}

NetCage can refuse Wi-Fi and mobile connections for a selected Android app while the rest of the phone stays online.

## What else can NetCage do? \{#netcage-fact-2}

A rule can target Wi-Fi, mobile data or both.
