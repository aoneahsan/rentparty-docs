Your seat follows you. Sign in with Google to play online, keep cloud saves and earn coins.

Pass-and-play and CPU games work without signing in, and they’re saved on this device.

## How do you sign in? {#sign-in}

Choose Sign in and pick your Google account. Google is the only sign-in, so there’s no Rent Party password to forget.

Google opens in its own window. If your browser blocks it, allow pop-ups for the site, then try again. On another device, sign in with the same Google account and your online games, cloud saves, coins and collection are waiting.

## What happens the first time? {#first-time}

A short setup follows your first sign-in, and you can skip any step for now:

- **Your name.** What should the table call you? It shows on the board, in chat and on your public card.
- **Your token.** It walks the board for you. More tokens wait in the shop, for coins you earn by playing.
- **Notifications.** Turn them on to hear when it’s your turn, when you’re invited and when the table votes on a pause.
- **An invite code.** A friend’s invite code counts if you enter it within an hour of signing up.

## What’s on your profile? {#profile}

Your display name, avatar, stats and badges, the token, board, dice and frame you have equipped, and a link to your public card. Everyone sees your display name.

**The leaderboard** is off until you switch it on. The leaderboard then shows your display name, token, rating, rank and number of rated games to anyone, signed in or not. Your games count toward your rating either way.

## Where do alerts go? {#inbox-and-notifications}

Every alert lands in your **Inbox**: turn alerts, invites and plan news. From there, three settings decide what else reaches you, under Settings, Notifications.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Push notifications | Your turn, invites, pause votes and results, on every device you allow. |
| Quiet hours | Alerts wait until quiet hours end. Game clocks don’t stop, so your turn timer keeps running. Quiet hours and dates follow your time zone. |
| Email | Turn alerts email you only if you haven’t acted after 6 hours. Payment, plan, final-call and deletion emails always arrive. |

If notifications are blocked in your phone’s or browser’s settings, allow them for Rent Party there, then come back. [Why didn’t I get an alert?](./help.md#alerts) covers the rest.

## Friends, blocks and reports {#friends-and-safety}

When a friend opens a room for friends, it’s listed for you on Play online.

**Blocking** someone ends your friendship. You won’t see each other’s chat, and neither of you can join a room the other hosts or send a friend request. They can’t watch games you play in. Games you’re already in together carry on.

**Reporting**: you can report someone’s profile, a chat message or a room. Choose Report on it, pick a reason and add details if you like. Reports are private both ways. They never learn who reported them, and you won’t see the outcome.

## Can you download your data? {#your-data}

Yes. In Settings, under Your data, **Download my data** gives you one file with everything tied to your account: profile, settings, stats, coins, collection, badges, plan history, payments, friends, blocks, game history and messages to the team.

Rent Party counts anonymous use of the game to make it better. The details are in the [Privacy Policy](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/privacy).

## How do you delete your account? {#delete-account}

From Settings, or on the [Delete account page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/delete-account). You confirm with Google, and the account is deleted at once and can’t be undone. We email you when it’s done. To keep a copy of your data, download it first.

- **Deleted at once:** your profile, cloud saves, saved replays, friends, blocks, coins, collection, daily-challenge progress and push subscriptions, voice recordings and AI allowance counts. A paid plan ends with the account.
- **What stays:** finished online games keep your seat as “Former player”. Payment records stay only as long as tax law requires, with no game data. Reports you made or received keep an anonymous id. A recap you asked for stays with its game for the rest of the table, with no link to you.

Online games still running or paused count as a forfeit for you, and the rest of the table plays on. If you own a Family plan, make another member its owner first, or the Family ends with your account and its members go back to their own plans.

The Delete account page lists everything that’s deleted and everything that stays.
