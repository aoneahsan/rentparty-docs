Before a game starts, pick a preset or switch house rules on one at a time. They’re set at the House rules step when you [start a game](../intro.md#start-a-game).

:::note[Numbers on this page]

They match the game’s current settings. The game’s own [How to play](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/how-to-play) page always shows the latest.

:::

## Which presets can you pick? {#presets}

| Preset | What it sets |
|---|---|
| Official | The classic rules, with every house rule off. |
| Classic family | The piggy bank, the Start-landing bonus and trades any time. |
| Quick | The short game described under [winning](./rules.md#winning). |
| Tournament | Official, with turn and decision timers always enforced. |

## Which house rules can you switch on? {#house-rules}

| House rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| Piggy bank | Taxes, fees and every card payment to the bank go in, repairs included, and whoever lands on Town Square takes it all. |
| Start-landing bonus | An extra 200 bucks for landing exactly on Start. |
| Turn-only trading | You can only trade on your own turn. |
| Game length limit | A set number of rounds or minutes, then the highest net worth wins. |
| No auctions | A street nobody buys stays with the bank. |
| Starting bucks and salary | Choose your own amounts. |
| City-tax choice | Land on City tax and pay either 200 bucks or 10% of your net worth, and you have to pick one before you count. |
| Sealed bids | For live and local games, as [the auction section](./rules.md#auctions) explains. Around one device, everyone types a bid in secret, passing the device between bids. |
| Quick deal | Everyone starts with 2 random deeds from the bank. |

If your family always piled the taxes in the middle of the board and handed the pile to whoever stopped on the quiet corner, the piggy bank is that rule.

## Which rules can a rated game use? {#rated-games}

Only Official or Tournament. A rated online game counts toward your rating when every seat is a person. [Online play](../online-play.md#rated-games) explains ratings.
