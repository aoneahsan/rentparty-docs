Every name that’s ours, in one place.

## On the board {#on-the-board}

| Rent Party word | What it means |
|---|---|
| street | A deed in a color set. Houses and hotels go here. |
| deed | The card that proves you own a street, station or utility. |
| station · utility | The 4 stations and 2 utilities you can own. |
| Start | The first corner, where you collect your salary. |
| Jail · just passing by | The second corner, and what you are when an ordinary roll lands you there. |
| Town Square | The rest corner, home of the piggy bank house rule. |
| Go to jail | The fourth corner. It sends you to Jail. |
| City tax · Party tax | The two tax spaces. |
| Lucky Break · Neighborhood Mail | The two card decks. |
| Jail pass | The card that gets you out of Jail. Keep it or trade it. |
| bucks | The play money in every game. |
| coins | Earned in online and CPU games, or got in a coin pack on the website. Spent on tokens, boards and dice skins, put in at coin tables, or sent to another player. In-app only, with no cash value, and separate from bucks. [Coins](../coins.md) has the details. |
| Kettleford | The board that comes with the game. |

## Around the game {#around-the-game}

| Rent Party word | What it means |
|---|---|
| CPU characters | The computer opponents, each with a name and a personality. [Meet them](./cpu-characters.md). |
| game invite | An invite to a game room: a link, a QR code or a 6-character room code. |
| Family invite | An invite to a seat on a Family plan, sent by email. |
| friend invite | An invite to Rent Party itself. Confirmed ones earn coins, a badge and [months of Pro](../plans-and-pricing.md#friend-invites). |
