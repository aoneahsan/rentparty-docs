Setup runs in the order below. We tell you at each step what we need next.

## What happens right after I pay? {#after-paying}

You pay by Wise transfer from our payment page and send us the transfer reference. Once we've confirmed the transfer, we email your license key and its certificate to the address you bought with.

## What do you need from me? {#what-we-need}

1. **Your domain**, like `play.yourbrand.com`. You add the DNS record we send you.
2. **Your name and logo**, as files we can put on the board's corner and the app icon.
3. **Your colors**, if you have brand colors you want used.

## When is it live? {#live}

Once your domain answers and your brand is applied, we deploy it and tell you it's live. Your deployment carries its license key and a license file anyone can [check](./verify-a-license.md).

## What if I want changes during setup? {#changes}

Changes made only for you are custom work, quoted on their own. Send them from the [custom work page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work) and we'll reply by email.
