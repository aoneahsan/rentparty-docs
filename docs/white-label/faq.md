## Do I get the source code with a license? {#source-code}

No. With a license we host and run Rent Party under your brand. The source code changes hands only with a [buyout](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout).

## Can someone else license Rent Party too? {#others}

Yes, until someone buys it outright. After a buyout we stop selling it, and licenses sold before then keep their terms.

## Is there an iPhone app? {#ios}

The mobile platform covers Android and iOS. We deliver iOS once you provide or fund an Apple developer account.

## Do several platforms cost less together? {#bundle}

Yes. Two or more platforms come with a bundle discount. The [prices page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing) shows the total for the platforms you pick.

## Can I put another game's name on it? {#trademarks}

No. The name, logo and artwork you use must be yours to use. A license or a buyout never covers another company's trademarks, game names or artwork.

## How do my players know it's licensed? {#licensed}

Your deployment carries its license key, and anyone can [check it](./verify-a-license.md).

## Where are the terms for payments? {#payments}

In the [Terms of Service](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/terms#what-do-white-label-licenses-and-buyouts-cover) and the [Privacy Policy](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/privacy#white-label-buyers).
