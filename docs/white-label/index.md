A white-label license puts your name on Rent Party. We host it, deploy it, connect your domain and apply your name, logo and colors, and you offer it to your own players. They get the whole game: live tables, games over days, pass-and-play and CPU characters.

The current prices, and the total for the platforms you pick, are on the [white-label prices page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/pricing). This section explains what you get; that page is the one to quote from.

## What's the difference between a license and a buyout? {#license-or-buyout}

A **license** is hosted by us. No source code changes hands, and others may license Rent Party too until someone buys it outright.

A **buyout** hands you the source code of every repository that builds the web app and the Android app. After a buyout we stop selling Rent Party to anyone else, and licenses sold before it keep their terms. The [buyout page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout) has the details and the price.

## Which platforms can I license? {#platforms}

The web app and the mobile app, alone or together. The mobile platform covers Android and iOS; we deliver iOS once you provide or fund an Apple developer account. [What you get on each platform](./delivered.md) lists what ships with each one.

## Can I use my own name and artwork? {#your-brand}

Yes, and it has to be yours. A license never covers another company's game names, trademarks or artwork, and we won't apply them.

## Can you change the game for my players? {#custom-work}

Yes. A house rule of your own, a board for your city or a feature nobody else has is custom work, quoted and paid on its own. Ask on the [custom work page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

## Where are the full terms? {#terms}

The [license in plain words](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/license-terms) is the short version, and the [Terms of Service](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/terms#what-do-white-label-licenses-and-buyouts-cover) are the full agreement.
