Every license and buyout includes six months of updates from the day you buy. A renewal adds more.

## What counts as an update? {#what-counts}

Everything we ship to Rent Party itself: fixes, rules and engine improvements, new features and new City boards. Changes made only for you are custom work.

## What happens when my updates end? {#when-they-end}

Your Rent Party keeps running on the last version it had. Nothing is switched off.

## How do I renew? {#renew}

Pick a renewal plan on the [updates page](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates), then enter your license key and the email the license was bought with. Plans add 3, 6 or 12 months; that page shows each plan's current price.

## When does a renewal start? {#start}

From whichever is later: today, or the day your current updates end. Renewing early never shortens what you already have.
