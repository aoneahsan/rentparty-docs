Each platform you license runs the same game as Rent Party, under your name. The rules, the board engine and the game modes are the ones on rentparty.aoneahsan.com today.

## What comes with the web app? {#web}

The whole game in any browser, on a domain you choose: live online tables, games that run one turn at a time over days, pass-and-play on one device and CPU characters to play against. A game can be paused and finished later, with every seat saved. We host it and ship each update to it.

## What comes with the mobile app? {#mobile}

The Android app, built under your name, with the same game as the web app. The mobile platform covers iOS too: we deliver the iOS app once you provide or fund an Apple developer account. Store accounts you choose to open, like your own Google Play developer account, are yours.

## What changes with my brand? {#brand}

Your name, logo and colors appear where Rent Party's are now, and your domain replaces ours. Changes beyond that, such as a new rule or your own board, are [custom work](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/custom-work).

## Is anything left out? {#left-out}

A license doesn't include the source code; only a [buyout](https://rentparty.aoneahsan.com/white-label/buyout) does. Paid services you choose to add, and your domain, are yours to pay for.
